Ten figures on a watch face, every corner cut

Arris began as the figures of the Dymaxion watch face, drawn on a grid of 9 × 12 pixels. Every outer corner was cut at 45 degrees, the notch in the 2 and the waist in the 3 were kept, and a whole alphabet grew in the same language.

The watch keeps your time. ss20 draws the figures as the watch did, one square per pixel. Between figures, calt sets the watch's time colon.

Every glyph on the figures' 10-module pitch, and 45 ligatures that never move the grid: a sequence of characters becomes one sign drawn back across their cells, each character still where it stands.


      

    

  

  
Edit the code. Ligatures form as you type (calt, on by default; editors can turn it off). Cells shows the grid every character keeps.


  
The ligatures

  

  
Hover or tap a sign to see its characters, outlined in red, exactly where they stand. Each is as many cells wide as the characters it replaces.

  

    Never forms
    
  

  
A ligature never forms when another operator touches it, so <=<, >>= and ----> stay as their characters. Sequences that would only be drawn closer together (:: // || ..) are left alone.


  
In the terminal

  

  
Box drawing, blocks, shades, 256 Braille cells and the Powerline glyphs fill their cells and join row to row.

The italic is drawn, not slanted: ten degrees, every chamfer a curve, a single-storey a and a descending f, while every point the upright keeps sharp stays sharp.

  






  
Built from code

  
Every glyph in Arris is a construction in code, drawn from the watch's pixel masters, measured instead of eyeballed, and checked before each release.

  
    
    
  • Strokes certified: no crossing outlines, nothing below the stroke floor, no swelling, upright and italic.
    • 
    
  • Openings kept: counters that would close at heavy weights are found and opened.
    • 
    
  • Interpolation checked: 7,667 places per glyph in the variable fonts, so no weight in between swings past the drawings.
    • 
    
  • Fontbakery: no FAILs and no ERRORs on the static, variable and Mono fonts.
    • 
  

  
The drawing engine, the build and every check are open, under the same licence as the fonts. github.com/huntrontrakkr/arris-type
